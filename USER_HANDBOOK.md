# User Handbook
**Gloomhaven: Jaws of the Lion - Unofficial Companion App**

Welcome to the unofficial companion app for *Gloomhaven: Jaws of the Lion*. This tool is designed to streamline your campaign management, track character progression, and assist with scenario setup and conclusion.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Installation
This app is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. You can use it directly in your browser or install it to your device for an app-like experience.
- **Desktop (Chrome/Edge):** Click the "Install" icon in the address bar.
- **Mobile (iOS Safari):** Tap "Share" → "Add to Home Screen".
- **Mobile (Android Chrome):** Tap "Menu" → "Install App".

### Your First Campaign
1.  On the home screen, you will see a welcome message.
2.  Tap **"+ New Campaign"** (or use the open creation form if it's your first time).
3.  Enter a name for your campaign (e.g., "The Lion's Guard") and press Enter or click Create.

---

## 2. Managing Campaigns

The home screen displays your campaigns.

- **Active Campaign:** The campaign you are currently playing is highlighted at the top with detailed progress stats. Click "Continue Campaign" to jump in.
- **Switching Campaigns:** Scroll down to the "All Campaigns" list and tap any campaign to switch to it.
- **Deleting a Campaign:** Tap the "×" icon on a campaign card. You will be asked to confirm before the data is permanently erased.

---

## 3. Campaign Dashboard

Once inside a campaign, you'll see the **Campaign Dashboard**. The header shows your overall progress (scenarios completed vs. total).

### The "Party" Tab
This is your main hub for character management.
- **Party Members:** Shows all characters in your party (up to 4).
- **Adding Characters:** Use the form at the bottom of the list. Select a class (Hatchet, Red Guard, Demolitionist, Voidwarden), enter a name, and tap "Add Character".
- **What's Next:** A smart card that tells you exactly which scenario to play next based on your unlocks.

### The "Scenarios" Tab
This tab tracks the state of every scenario in the game.
- **Locked (Gray):** You cannot play this yet.
- **Unlocked (Amber):** Available to play. Tap to mark as completed.
- **Completed (Green/Strikethrough):** Already finished. Tap to revert to unlocked if needed.
- **Goals:** Each card displays the scenario's objective (e.g., "Kill all enemies").

---

## 4. Character Sheet

Tap any character card in the Party list to open their **Character Sheet**.

- **Stats:** Manage Level, Experience (XP), and Gold.
  - *Note:* Leveling up automatically increases your HP and adjusts your Perk count.
- **Checkmarks:** Track battle goal checkmarks. Every 3 checkmarks grant a Perk.
- **Perks:** Select perks as you earn them. The app validates that you don't exceed your earned perks (Level - 1 + Checkmark Perks).
- **Items:**
  - **Shop:** Browse all items in the game. Items are locked by default and unlock as your "City Event" deck progresses or scenarios reward them.
  - **Inventory:** Buy items (deducts gold) or Sell items (refunds half value).
  - **Head/Body/Feet/Hands:** Equipped items are sorted by slot.

---

## 5. Tools & Utilities

The Campaign Dashboard provides quick access to essential tools.

### Scenario Level Calculator
*Located in the "Tools" section of the Party tab.*
- **Party Overview:** Shows everyone's level and the calculated average.
- **Difficulty Selector:** Choose Easy (-1), Normal (0), Hard (+1), or Very Hard (+2).
- **Results:**
  - **Scenario Level:** The final level to set your monster decks and stat cards to.
  - **Stats:** Instantly see Monster Level, Trap Damage, Gold Conversion rate, and Bonus XP for the scenario.

### Post-Scenario Checklist
*Located in the "Tools" section of the Party tab.*
- Use this after finishing a game session.
- **Outcome:** Toggle between **Success** (Won) or **Failure** (Lost). The checklist steps update automatically.
- **Gold Calculator:** Enter the number of money tokens you looted. The app calculates the total gold based on the scenario level.
- **Step-by-Step Guide:** Follow the list to ensure you don't miss anything (e.g., adding city events, recording XP, battle goals).

---

## 6. Rules & Reference

Access the **Rules** section via the bottom navigation bar.

- **Glossary:** A searchable dictionary of game terms (e.g., "Muddle", "Jump", "Advantage").
- **Reference:** Digital reference cards for round structure, condition tokens, and elemental infusions.
- **Focus Helper:** An interactive tool to determine monster focus.
  1.  **Question 1:** Can the monster attack its current target?
  2.  **Question 2:** Is there a path to a hex from which it can attack?
  3.  **Result:** The tool guides you to the correct focus logic.

---

## 7. Settings & Data

Access **Settings** via the bottom navigation bar.

- **Data Management:**
  - **Export Data:** Download a `.json` backup of all your campaigns. Useful for moving to a new device.
  - **Import Data:** Restore a backup file.
  - **Reset App:** **Warning:** This permanently deletes all campaigns and resets the app to a fresh state.
- **About:** Displays version information (`v1.0.0`) and license details.
