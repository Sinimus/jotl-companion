### Universal Rule: Players Decide Ambiguity

If players ever arrive at a situation where there are two or more equally viable outcomes and the rules offer no definitive answer, players decide among themselves what happens. This typically arises during monster movement when a monster has two or more paths to its focus that are of equal length.

### Glossary of Terms (A-Z)

#### A

- **A (Level)**: Character ability cards designated as level "A" are used for the first three scenarios. Each character starts Scenario 1 with 6 level "A" cards.
    
- **Ability**: A single process a figure performs, usually on their turn. Multiple abilities make up an action and are separated by a line on the card.
    
- **Ability Card (Character)**: Decks representing character stamina and actions. Two cards are played each round; one for its top action and one for its bottom action.
    
- **Ability Card (Monster)**: A deck of eight cards used by monsters to determine initiative and actions for the round.
    
- **Action**: The entire half (top or bottom) of a character ability card performed as a whole.
    
- **Activation Tokens**: Placed on door hexes to show they are open or used for specific scenario rules.
    
- **Active Area**: Area above a character mat for tracking persistent ability bonuses.
    
- **Adjacent**: Hexes sharing a border. Figures in these hexes are adjacent. Wall lines block adjacency.
    
- **Advantage/Disadvantage**:
    
    - **Advantage**: Reveal two modifier cards and apply the best.
        
    - **Disadvantage**: Reveal two modifier cards and apply the worst. Occurs from MUDDLE or ranged attacks on adjacent targets.
        
- **Ally**: A figure fighting on the same side of a battle. You are not your own ally.
    
- **Area Effect**: Depicts an area of red hexes that can be targeted. A grey hex indicates the attacker's required position for non-ranged abilities.
    
- **Attack (Ability)**: Targets suffer base damage $X$ modified by attack bonuses, one modifier card, and target Shield.
    
- **Attack Bonus**: Bonuses received from conditions, items, or persistent abilities. Attackers receive +1 Attack against poisoned targets.
    
- **Attack Modifier Card**: Drawn during an attack to modify damage. Decks contain special "2x" and "Null" cards.
    

#### B

- **B (Level)**: Character cards used for the second and third scenarios. Replaced by level "1" cards after Scenario 3.
    
- **Basic Action**: Using the top of any card for "Attack 2" or the bottom for "Move 2".
    
- **Battle Goal**: Secret objectives chosen at the start of a scenario. Successful completion yields check marks.
    
- **Bless**: A condition that shuffles a $2\times$ card into the figure's modifier deck.
    
- **Boss**: Powerful monsters with unique actions. Stats scale based on the number of characters ($C$).
    
- **Boss Specials**: Descriptions of unique boss actions found in the Scenario Book.
    

#### C

- **C**: Shorthand for the number of characters that started the scenario.
    
- **Campaign**: The series of 17 scenarios following the game's story.
    
- **Card Selection**: The first part of every round where players select two cards or declare a long rest.
    
- **Character**: Playable avatars (Red Guard, Demolitionist, Hatchet, Voidwarden).
    
- **Character Mat**: Board specifying hand limit, maximum HP, and card placement areas.
    
- **Character Sheet**: Used to track level, XP, gold, items, perks, and check marks.
    
- **Character Token**: Used to track persistent ability uses or other effects.
    
- **Check Mark**: Earned from battle goals and scenario rewards. Three check marks earn one perk.
    
- **City Map Board**: Visual record of completed and unlocked scenarios using stickers.
    
- **Conclusion**: Thematic wrap-up read only after scenario success.
    
- **Condition**: Effects (e.g., STUN, POISON) that change how a figure functions. Most remain until the end of the figure's next turn.
    
- **Curse**: A condition that shuffles a "Null" card into the figure's modifier deck.
    

#### D

- **Damage**: Reductions in current hit points.
    
- **Destroy Obstacle (Ability)**: Removes an obstacle and replaces it with destruction tokens.
    
- **Destruction Tokens**: Placed when obstacles or objectives are destroyed to show the hex is now empty.
    
- **Dial**: Used by characters to track current HP (red) and accumulated XP (blue).
    
- **Difficult Terrain**: Hexes with purple borders requiring 2 movement points to enter.
    
- **Difficulty**: Scenario level adjusted for easy, normal, hard, or very hard play.
    
- **Disarm**: Condition preventing all Attack abilities.
    
- **Discard Pile**: Area for used character cards or revealed modifier cards.
    
- **Door**: Hexes with blue borders separating rooms. Moving onto one opens it and reveals the next room.
    
- **Dot**: Small dots on cards to be ignored; used for compatibility with the larger Gloomhaven game.
    
- **Dotted Line**: Ability card lines separating individual abilities within an action.
    

#### E

- **Elements**: A combo system involving Fire, Ice, Air, Earth, Light, and Dark. Infused at end of turn; consumed to augment abilities.
    
- **Elite (Monster)**: More powerful monsters placed in gold stands.
    
- **Empty Hex**: A hex with no figure, obstacle, objective, trap, or difficult terrain.
    
- **End of Round**: The final phase where modifiers are shuffled and elements wane.
    
- **End-of-Turn Looting**: Characters automatically loot their current hex at the end of their turn.
    
- **Enemy**: A figure on the opposite side of the battle.
    
- **Event**: Collective choices made after successful scenarios by drawing from the event deck.
    
- **Exhaustion**: Occurs when HP reaches 0 or cards cannot be played. Figure is removed from the map.
    
- **Experience (XP)**: Earned by performing actions with XP icons or expending persistent ability uses.
    

#### F

- **Figure**: Any monster or character on the map.
    
- **Focus**: The enemy a monster can reach for an attack with the least movement. Tie-breakers use initiative order.
    

#### G

- **Goal**: Specific conditions required to successfully complete a scenario.
    
- **Gold**: Currency used to buy items, gained by collecting money tokens.
    
- **Grant Action (Ability)**: Forcing or allowing another figure to perform an action on your turn.
    

#### H

- **Hand**: Ability cards available to be played at the start of a round.
    
- **Heal (Ability)**: Removes damage from monsters or increases character HP.
    
- **Hex**: A single hexagon on the map; the game's unit of spatial measurement.
    
- **Hit Points (HP)**: Tracking a figure's life; reduced by damage and increased by healing.
    

#### I

- **Immobilize**: Condition preventing all Move abilities.
    
- **Initiative**: Determines the turn order within a round. Lowest value acts first.
    
- **Initiative Order Token**: Tokens used to track the act order for characters and monster sets.
    
- **Introduction**: Thematic lead-in text for a scenario.
    
- **Item**: Cards providing bonuses, usually limited in use (Consumed or Spent).
    

#### J

- **Jump**: Movement modifier that ignores enemies, obstacles, traps, and difficult terrain. Walls still block movement.
    

#### K

- **Key**: Reference in the Scenario Book for monsters, traps, and treasure present.
    
- **Kill**: Removing a monster standee after its HP reaches 0.
    
- **Kill Credit**: Determining which character receives credit for a monster's death for battle goals.
    

#### L

- **L**: Shorthand for the scenario level.
    
- **Level (Character)**: Progress determined by XP, granting new cards, perks, and higher HP.
    
- **Level (Scenario)**: Average character level divided by 2 (rounded up), modified by difficulty.
    
- **Line-of-Sight**: Requirement for targeting; established if a line can be drawn hex-to-hex without touching a wall.
    
- **Loot (Ability)**: Ability to pick up money and treasure within range.
    
- **Lost (Character Ability Cards)**: Cards moved to a pile that cannot be used for the rest of the scenario.
    
- **Lost (Scenario)**: Occurs if all characters are exhausted or specific loss conditions are met.
    

#### M

- **Melee**: An attack where "Range" is not used; targets must be adjacent.
    
- **Money Token**: Tokens placed on the map to be converted into gold after a scenario.
    
- **Monster**: Figures that are enemies to characters, represented by standees.
    
- **Monster Movement**: Monsters move to get within range of their focus with minimum movement.
    
- **Move (Ability)**: Spending movement points to travel between hexes.
    
- **Muddle**: Condition causing Disadvantage on all attacks.
    

#### N

- **Negating Damage**: Characters can lose one card from their hand or two from discard to prevent all damage from one source.
    
- **Normal (Monster)**: Standard rank monsters in white stands.
    

#### O

- **Objective**: Yellow-bordered hexes that must be destroyed or protected.
    
- **Obstacle**: Green-bordered hexes that cannot be moved through unless using Jump.
    
- **Occupied**: A hex containing a figure.
    
- **Order of Round**: Sequence: 1) Card Selection, 2) Ordering of Initiative, 3) Turns, 4) End of Round.
    
- **Ordering of Initiative**: Revealing selected cards and monster cards to arrange turn order.
    

#### P

- **Perk**: Permanent attack modifier deck improvements gained by leveling up or check marks.
    
- **Persistent (Ability)**: Active bonuses kept in the active area for a specified time or number of uses.
    
- **Poison**: Condition causing all attacks against the figure to gain +1 Attack. Prevents healing from increasing HP.
    
- **Pull (Ability)**: Forcing an enemy up to $X$ hexes closer to the figure using the ability.
    
- **Push (Ability)**: Forcing an enemy up to $X$ hexes farther away from the figure using the ability.
    

#### R

- **Range**: Targeted abilities with "Range X" can target anything within $X$ hexes in line-of-sight.
    
- **Resting**: Returning cards from discard to hand.
    
    - **Short Rest**: Done at end of round; one random card is lost.
        
    - **Long Rest**: Declared during card selection; initiative 99; player chooses card to lose and heals 2.
        
- **Revealing a Room**: Opening a door to set up monsters and determine their initiative.
    
- **Rewards**: Benefits (gold, XP, items) received for completing a scenario.
    
- **Room**: Discrete areas separated by doors and walls.
    
- **Round**: A unit of time where every figure on the map takes one turn.
    

#### S

- **Saving**: Storing character data and map progress between sessions.
    
- **Scenario**: Self-contained game sessions forming the campaign.
    
- **Section Break**: Pause points in scenario text read only when specific conditions are met.
    
- **Self**: An ability targeting only the figure using it.
    
- **Shield (Ability)**: Active bonus reducing attack damage taken.
    
- **Shop**: Deck of available items characters can purchase.
    
- **Shuffle**: Icon indicating a deck's discard pile is shuffled back in at the end of the round.
    
- **Spawn**: Monsters appearing via scenario rules; they act in the round they are spawned.
    
- **Special Rules**: Additional rules unique to a specific scenario.
    
- **Standee**: Cardboard representation of a monster.
    
- **Stat Card**: Card providing base monster stats for different levels and ranks.
    
- **Stat Envelope**: Holder for stat cards that tracks damage and conditions for a monster set.
    
- **Sticker**: Used on the map board to track scenario availability and progress.
    
- **Strengthen**: Condition causing Advantage on all attacks.
    
- **Stun**: Condition preventing a figure from performing any abilities on its turn.
    
- **Suffer Damage**: Directly reducing HP; cannot be reduced by Shield.
    
- **Supplemental Scenario Book**: Book containing extra map areas or text for larger scenarios.
    

#### T

- **Target**: The figure(s) an ability is performed on. Must be in line-of-sight.
    
- **Trap**: Map features that apply damage or conditions when a figure moves into them.
    
- **Treasure Tile**: Map features acquired via looting to receive unique rewards.
    
- **Turn**: A figure's specific action period within a round.
    

#### W

- **Wall**: Thick dark lines on the map that cannot be crossed and block line-of-sight.
    
- **Wound**: Condition causing the figure to suffer 1 damage at the start of each of its turns.
    

#### X

- **X (Level)**: Extra character cards providing deck-building flexibility.

### Appendix G: Monster Movement & Focus Logic

#### 1. Finding a Focus

- At the start of its turn, a monster finds a focus, which is the enemy it can get in range to attack using the least movement.
    
- This is independent of line-of-sight or current attack ability.
    
- Monsters calculate paths through the fewest possible traps.
    
- Tie-breakers for focus use the earliest initiative order.
    

#### 2. Monster Movement

- Monsters move to get in range for their focus and maximize their attack.
    
- They move the minimum amount required to maximize this attack.
    
- Ranged monsters move away from their focus to avoid Disadvantage from adjacency if possible.
    
- If a monster is already in range and lacks Disadvantage, it will not move.
    
- Players decide movement for any equal viable options.

### Appendix H: Line-of-Sight (LoS)

- Targeted abilities require the target to be in line-of-sight.
    
- LoS exists if a line can be drawn from any part of the attacker's hex to any part of the target's hex without touching a wall line.
    
- Obstacles, objectives, and other figures do not block line-of-sight; only walls do.
    
- Closed doors act as walls for LoS until they are opened.
